Brand Philosophy

The fashion industry throws away a truck of clothes every second. We think the cloth is still good, so we cut, patch and re-sew it into something you will keep.

Sustainable development, for us, means three things: no new fabric where old fabric will do, fair pay for the people who sew, and repair for life on everything we sell.

Upcycling Process

  1. Collect

    Donated clothing arrives at our studio and is sorted by fabric and condition.

  2. Wash and deconstruct

    Seams are opened, buttons saved, and the cloth is washed.

  3. Redesign

    Panels are laid out by hand so each garment gets its own pattern.

  4. Sew and number

    Each piece is sewn in Almaty and gets a number in its collar.
